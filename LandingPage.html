Why MetaStack?

MVP Prototyping

MetaStack is built for rapid prototyping. Whether you're validating an idea or building a Minimum Viable Product (MVP), MetaStack helps you go from concept to reality in record time. No unnecessary complexity—just the tools you need to ship fast.

HTML-Based Coding

With MetaStack, you write plain HTML enhanced by TailwindCSS and AlpineJS. No need to learn complex frameworks or templating engines. Just focus on building your app with familiar, straightforward code.

Easier CSS

Say goodbye to messy CSS files. MetaStack uses TailwindCSS, a utility-first CSS framework that lets you style directly in your HTML. It's fast, intuitive, and keeps your codebase clean and maintainable.

Easier Backend

MetaStack simplifies backend development with ExpressJS and MongoDB. No bloated frameworks or unnecessary abstractions—just a lightweight, powerful backend that gets the job done.

Zen Minimalism

MetaStack embraces minimalism. No wasted time on over-engineering or unnecessary features. It's designed to help you focus on what matters: building your product.

MicroSaaS & Single-Page Apps

MetaStack is perfect for MicroSaaS projects and single-page applications. Build one feature at a time, iterate quickly, and scale effortlessly with a cloud-native architecture.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MetaStack?

MetaStack is a modern web development boilerplate designed for rapid prototyping and building MVPs. It combines MongoDB, ExpressJS, TailwindCSS, and AlpineJS for a seamless development experience.

Is MetaStack free?

Yes, MetaStack is completely free and open-source. You can use it for personal or commercial projects.

Can I use MetaStack for production?

Absolutely! MetaStack is lightweight and production-ready. It’s perfect for building MicroSaaS apps and single-page applications.

How do I get started?

Simply clone the repository, install dependencies, and start coding! Check out the CTA Section for detailed instructions.
